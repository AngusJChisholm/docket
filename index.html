How it works

About ten minutes on a Sunday arvo.

  1. Tell it who's eating. How many people, how many dinners, your budget, your store and anything you won't eat. Gluten free is handled with swaps.
  2. Pick your dinners. Butter chicken, roast chook, miso salmon, lamb cutlets, poke bowls and plenty more, each one priced per serve.
  3. Shop and cook. One list sorted by aisle, plus the recipes. Cook double and tomorrow's lunch is done.

Three kinds of dinner

Most weeks we cook three everyday dinners, one premium night and one lighter one. Mix yours however you like.

    Started in a share house kitchen

    We were five blokes in a Melbourne share house paying a meal kit to feed us. One night someone did the maths: about ten bucks a serve, plus delivery, plus a mountain of cardboard.

    So we started doing it ourselves. Same sort of dinners, bigger serves, cooked Sunday to Thursday with leftovers for lunch. All five of us eat well for under $400 a week.

    Questions

    Is it free?

    Yes, while we're in beta. You plan, we build the list. No account, no card.

    How accurate are the prices?

    Right now they're our estimates for Melbourne stores. Live weekly Coles and Woolies prices are on the way, refreshed every Wednesday when the specials change.

    Does it work if I'm cooking for one or two?

    Yes. Riversdale Fresh picks the smallest packs that cover what you need, prices meat and veg by weight, shows what you'll have left over, and suggests a dinner that uses it up. Anything left in the pantry carries over to next week rather than counting against this one.

    Do you deliver the food?

    No box, no delivery. You shop at your usual supermarket with our list, which is where the savings come from. A one-tap "send to my trolley" for Coles and Woolies is on our list.

    Can I do gluten free?

    Yes. Tell us how many gluten free eaters you've got and we add the swaps (GF pasta, buns, crumbs, tamari) in the right amounts. Always check labels on sauces and spice mixes.

    Why compare yourselves to meal kits?

    Because that's what we were paying for. We use each kit's lowest published price for the same number of serves, delivery included, and we'll say so when a budget kit comes in cheaper.

    Are you linked to any supermarket or meal kit?

    No. Riversdale Fresh is independent.
